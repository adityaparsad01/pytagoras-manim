# Animation Architecture

The animation is organized as two visual arguments.

## Part 1 — Concrete example

1. Introduce a right triangle.
2. Label the sides 3, 4 and 5.
3. Build squares on the three sides.
4. Show their areas.
5. Demonstrate 9 + 16 = 25.

## Part 2 — General proof

1. Create a large square of side a + b.
2. Place four congruent right triangles inside it.
3. Highlight the center square with area c².
4. Derive the area equation.
5. Cancel 2ab.
6. End with a² + b² = c².

The intention is to make the geometry explain the algebra rather than simply displaying an equation.
